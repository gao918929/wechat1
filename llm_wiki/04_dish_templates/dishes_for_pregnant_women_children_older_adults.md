# 孕妇、儿童与老人适宜菜 Dishes for Pregnant Women, Children & Older Adults

用量为**每人份**（克）。软烂易嚼、少盐少辣、营养密度高。

## 山药胡萝卜鸡汤
- en: Chicken Soup with Chinese Yam & Carrot
- kind: soup
- meals: lunch, dinner
- tags: soup, pregnancy_friendly, child_friendly, elderly_friendly, soft, stomach_friendly
- spicy: 0
- difficulty: easy
- minutes: 60
- ingredients: 鸡腿 100, 山药 100, 胡萝卜 50, 姜 3, 盐 1
- steps: 鸡腿焯水后与姜片炖 40 分钟，加入山药胡萝卜再炖 20 分钟，加盐。

- minutes: 15
