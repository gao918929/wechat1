# Dish structures (not ready-made generated plans)

- Home-style: one or more catalog vegetables plus a compatible protein, with a measured staple and explicit oil amount.
- Light meals: choose steaming, simmering or another individually tolerated preparation; no claims to treat a stomach condition.
- Dormitory quick meals: minimize preparation complexity; respect available ingredients and user preferences without assuming specialized appliances.
- Special-needs alternatives: retain only globally safe ingredients; provide distinct dishes when necessary and label intended recipients. All people still meet their confirmed targets.

Generate actual dishes dynamically from the supplied catalog, stock, budgets and feedback. Every ingredient mentioned in preparation, including oils, must appear in the ingredient array (plain water excepted). Do not add implicit sauces, nuts or garnishes. Do not quote a source for values that were not retrieved.
