# Nine dietary model families

1. Student dormitory meals: practical shared portions, varied affordable foods, explicit per-person budgets. Fat loss or muscle gain is a preference, never permission to invent a medical target.
2. Balanced family meals: diversity, common preferences, age-appropriate confirmed targets.
3. Light/stomach-friendly meals: favor individually tolerated preparation, not diagnosis or treatment.
4. Chronic-condition / blood-pressure / blood-glucose / blood-lipid management: require professionally confirmed targets and explicit contraindicated ingredient IDs. Do not prescribe medication or infer a disease-specific treatment.
5. Pregnancy and lactation: require confirmed targets; do not use ordinary adult auto-estimation. Hard exclusions always apply to every dish. Additional acceptable dishes may be offered to different diners; explain labels without revealing diagnoses.
6. Child growth: confirmed age-appropriate targets only, no adult deficit formula.
7. High-protein fitness meals: remain within confirmed protein/calorie bounds; do not assume more protein is always better.
8. Vegetarian: restrict to catalog entries marked vegetarian; users may add stricter exclusions for eggs or dairy.
9. Halal: restrict to explicitly compatible/certified catalog entries. Do not assume ordinary meat is certified.

Tags can be combined. Hard allergen and contraindication exclusions remain non-negotiable even when a population nutritional goal conflicts. If no feasible plan exists, report the conflicting inputs rather than inventing a safe option.
