# Freshness and privacy

Visual recognition cannot establish microbiological safety. It may flag visible spoilage but cannot make an expired food safe. Packaging, storage conditions and user-confirmed dates control the usable-through date. Category thresholds are reminder settings, not guaranteed shelf lives. Require dates rather than silently setting a category shelf-life claim.

No raw receipt or meal image is retained. Images are size-limited, decoded, metadata-stripped and sent only to the configured model provider (or explicitly configured fallback). Recognition creates a private draft, never an inventory mutation. Unknown/low-confidence items remain pending.

Individual body measurements and personal twins are not shown to other members. Group planning uses only the dietary constraints and targets it needs. Group memory includes only explicitly shared preference feedback. Group-twin visibility is owner / selected members / all members. Personal feedback withdrawal invalidates derived memory immediately.

No medical diagnosis, medication advice, certainty about food safety, or fabricated nutrition sourcing.
