# Genuine end-to-end demo script

Do not record test fixtures as if they were live AI responses. Before recording, configure the real DeepSeek account, real WeChat application and local backend. Use obviously labeled demonstration users rather than real health data.

1. Sign in, create a dormitory, copy the invitation code, and join from a second authenticated user.
2. Each user enters their own dietary constraints and confirms per-meal nutrition targets. Show that one member cannot read the other's private profile or twin.
3. Capture ingredient photos. Show the recognized draft, one uncertain weight labeled estimated, and a manual correction. Confirm edible weights and expiry dates.
4. Add sourced price references or recognize a real shopping receipt. Explicitly confirm the paid amount, then show inventory and the immutable ledger update together. Replay confirmation and verify no duplicate transaction.
5. Enter the specification's weekly-dinner request, selecting the actual members and per-person budget. Explain that structured allergies / contraindications must already be confirmed in profiles.
6. Display four independent agent stages, validation and real returned alternatives. Do not cut around an error and describe it as a success. Show trade-offs, sources and exact-vs-estimated prices.
7. Leave the page while generation is still running, then return through the in-app completion notification.
8. Choose an option. Show another member receiving the selected plan, and copy/export the shopping list after existing inventory has been removed.
9. Capture one home meal and confirm inventory usage. Capture an outside meal and verify that only intake changes.
10. Record a preference substitution, explain personal/group visibility, and generate another plan using the updated inputs. Weekly twin summaries refresh on access after seven days.

## What to say if credentials or permissions are missing

Show the actionable configuration error. Demonstrate manual inventory / receipts / permissions independently, and label the live AI / WeChat portions as pending. Do not hard-code a seven-day plan to hide the missing connection.
